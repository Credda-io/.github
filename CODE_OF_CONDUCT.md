# Code of Conduct

Credda is a small community. This is short on purpose.

## The standard

Be respectful and act in good faith. In any Credda space, whether that's issues,
pull requests, discussions, or email, we expect:

- Courtesy and patience, especially with people who know less than you do.
- Good-faith, constructive feedback aimed at ideas and code, not people.
- Respect for differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Graceful acceptance of a decision once it's been made.

We won't tolerate harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory or demeaning
language, sexualised content, intimidation, publishing others' private
information, or sustained disruption. This applies to everyone equally:
contributors, maintainers, and the Credda team.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces, and any time someone is representing the
project or its community in public.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to
**[martin@credda.io](mailto:martin@credda.io?subject=Code%20of%20conduct)** with
the subject "Code of conduct". It reaches the founder directly.

Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainers review every report and
respond as promptly as we reasonably can. Depending on severity we may warn,
remove content, or ban a participant temporarily or permanently. Maintainers who
don't uphold this code in good faith face the same consequences.
